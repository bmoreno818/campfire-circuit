Staying Connected Off-Grid

Plenty of great campsites have no signal. Here's how to stay safe and navigate without it.

1. Offline maps first

Before you leave, download the area in your mapping app. GPS works without cell service, but map tiles don't load without data unless you downloaded them ahead of time. Bring a paper map as a backup.

2. Satellite messaging

Some newer phones offer limited satellite emergency features. A dedicated communicator offers two-way messaging, location tracking, and SOS, and it doesn't depend on your phone's battery.
