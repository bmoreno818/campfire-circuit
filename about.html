About Campfire Circuit

Campfire Circuit publishes practical camping tips and guides to the tech that makes camp easier: power, water, light, and communication.

How we pick products

We feature gear based on its published specs and how well it fits real camping needs. We check specifications on the product page when we write each guide. We don't publish made-up ratings, testimonials, or prices, since prices change, so always check the current listing.

How we make money

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. That's how this site pays for itself, and it costs you nothing extra.
