Winter Camping Tech Checklist

Winter means long nights, dying batteries, and fewer people around if something goes wrong. Use this checklist before you head out.

Power

  • ☐ Bring more battery capacity than you would in summer, because cold reduces usable capacity.
  • ☐ Keep your power bank and phone in an inside pocket or your sleeping bag overnight.
  • ☐ Bring short cables. Cold cables get stiff and break.

Water

  • ☐ Keep hollow-fiber filters from freezing. Store them in your bag at night, and if one may have frozen, don't trust it. Bring a chemical backup.
  • ☐ Use insulated bottles, and store them upside down so ice forms at the bottom instead of in the lid.

Communication and safety

  • ☐ Download offline maps before you lose signal.
  • ☐ Leave your trip plan with someone at home.
  • ☐ Carry a satellite communicator for areas with no cell coverage.
